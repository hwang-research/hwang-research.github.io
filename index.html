Huan (Harry) Wang

Huan (Harry) Wang is an Assistant Professor of Management at Siena University (Ph.D., Rutgers University, 2024). His research is in behavioral ethics, management communication and applied artificial intelligence in organizations. This site holds his published work: his papers, the measurement scale he has developed, and open copies where the rights allow.

His publications appear under the names Harry Wang, Huan Wang, Harry H. Wang. ORCID: orcid.org/0000-0002-4318-7544. Faculty page: Siena University.

Scales

Duty to Dignity at Work (DDW)

Employees' volition to refrain from disrespecting others. It is a duty the employee holds toward other people at work, not a perception of how the employee is treated. The construct was introduced as Kantian duty orientation (KDO) in Wang (2024) and is named Duty to Dignity at Work (DDW) in Wang, Chen and Scharding (2025).
