Assistant Professor of Management, Siena University

Huan (Harry) Wang is an Assistant Professor of Management at Siena University (Ph.D., Rutgers University, 2024). His research is in behavioral ethics, management communication and applied artificial intelligence in organizations. This site holds his published work: his papers, the measurement scale he has developed, and open copies where the rights allow.

Scales

Measurement instruments documented in his published work.

Measurement scale

Duty to Dignity at Work (DDW)

Employees' volition to refrain from disrespecting others at work: a duty the employee holds toward others, not a perception of how the employee is treated. Introduced as Kantian duty orientation (KDO) in Wang (2024) and named Duty to Dignity at Work (DDW) in Wang, Chen and Scharding (2025).
