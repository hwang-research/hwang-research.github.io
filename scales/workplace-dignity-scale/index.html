Workplace Dignity Scale (WDS)

Dignity & EthicsValidated18 items

Employees' perceptions of dignity in the workplace, measured with 18 items (Thomas and Lucas 2019).

Theoretical roots

Developed from focus groups (N = 62), an expert panel (N = 11) and two surveys (Thomas and Lucas 2019).

Instrument

Permission required. The 18 items are published in Thomas and Lucas (2019), a Sage journal article, and are not reproduced here. Consult the article and the publisher's permission terms before reuse.

Psychometrics

Validity evidence

The authors report support for content, construct and criterion-related validity; workplace dignity explained variance in employee engagement, burnout and turnover intentions beyond organizational respect and meaningful work (abstract).

Validated in

  • Two survey samples, N = 401 and N = 542 (abstract)

Key references

  • Thomas, B., & Lucas, K. (2019). Development and Validation of the Workplace Dignity Scale. Group & Organization Management, 44(1), 72-111 · doi:10.1177/1059601118807784

Notes
