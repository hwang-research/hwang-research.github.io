Workplace Dignity Scale (WDS)

Dignity & EthicsValidated18 items

Employees' perceptions of dignity in the workplace, as measured by the 18-item Workplace Dignity Scale. The article's abstract says what the scale measures and does not state a one-sentence definition of workplace dignity; see the article for the authors' own conceptualisation.

Theoretical roots

Not stated in the abstract. The scale was developed from focus groups (N = 62), an expert panel (N = 11) and two surveys; see the article for its theoretical grounding.

Instrument

Permission required. The 18 items are published in Thomas and Lucas (2019), a Sage journal article, and are not reproduced here. Consult the article and the publisher's permission terms before reuse.

Psychometrics

Validity evidence

From the abstract: the results supported content, construct and criterion-related validity, and workplace dignity explained variance in employee engagement, burnout and turnover intentions beyond organizational respect and meaningful work. The abstract gives no reliability coefficient.

Validated in

  • Two survey samples, N = 401 and N = 542 (abstract)

Key references

  • Thomas, B., & Lucas, K. (2019). Development and Validation of the Workplace Dignity Scale. Group & Organization Management, 44(1), 72-111 · doi:10.1177/1059601118807784
